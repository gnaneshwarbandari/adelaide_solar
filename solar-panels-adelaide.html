How Solar Panels Work

1. Sunlight

Solar panels capture sunlight during daylight hours.

2. Solar Electricity

3. Inverter

The inverter converts the electricity for household or commercial use.

4. Energy Usage

Generated electricity can be used by your property or exported to the grid.
