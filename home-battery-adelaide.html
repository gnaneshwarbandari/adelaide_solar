Home Battery Storage Solutions in Adelaide

A home battery system allows you to store excess energy generated by your solar panels and use it when your household needs it. Instead of sending unused solar energy back to the grid, your battery can store that energy for later use.

Adelaide Solar & Electrical Services designs and installs residential battery storage systems to suit different homes, energy consumption patterns and existing solar installations.

Whether you already have solar panels or are planning a new solar and battery system, we can help you choose a practical solution for your property.

Benefits of Home Battery Storage

Make better use of your solar energy and improve your home's energy efficiency.

Store Excess Solar

Capture surplus solar energy generated during the day and save it for use later.

Reduce Electricity Costs

Use stored solar energy during periods when electricity from the grid may be more expensive.

Greater Energy Independence

Reduce your reliance on electricity from the grid by increasing the amount of energy you generate and store.

Designed for Your Home's Energy Needs

Every household uses energy differently. Battery sizing should consider your solar generation, household consumption, evening usage and future electricity requirements.

We assess your existing system and recommend a battery solution that works with your current or planned solar installation.

How Home Battery Storage Works

1. Solar Generation

Your solar panels generate electricity during daylight hours.

2. Home Usage

Your home uses the electricity generated by the solar system.

3. Battery Charging

Excess solar power can be stored in your battery.

4. Stored Energy

Stored energy can be used later when solar generation is lower.
