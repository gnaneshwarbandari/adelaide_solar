How Solar Battery Storage Works

A battery works alongside your solar system to store energy and make it available when required.

1
Solar Panels Generate Electricity

Your solar panels generate electricity during daylight hours.

2
Your Property Uses Solar

Solar electricity is first available to meet your property's energy requirements.

3
Excess Energy Is Stored

Depending on your system configuration, surplus solar energy can be stored in the battery.

4
Stored Energy Is Used Later

Stored energy can be used when solar generation is lower, depending on your system settings.
